ESP32C++BLEUltrasonic sensing

02AUTONOMOUS VEHICLES

Polaris GEM E2 Autonomous Driving

Our four-person UIUC ECE 484 team built a camera-based driving stack for a full-scale electric vehicle. I fine-tuned the lane segmentation model on GEM camera footage and helped turn its predictions into live waypoints. The car completed a two-lap on-track evaluation with stops for signs and pedestrians.

ROS 2PyTorchOpenCVPure Pursuit
Project notes +

The planner projected lane markings from image pixels onto the ground using camera calibration, then published lane-center waypoints. Pure Pursuit tracked those waypoints using wheel-speed and IMU odometry, while a safety supervisor could inhibit propulsion and request braking.

03EMBEDDED FIRMWARE

Mopbot

I owned the STM32 firmware for our two-person autonomous mopping robot. I wrote the C code for sensor acquisition, encoder feedback, PI wheel-speed control, and pump control that permits water delivery only during eligible forward cleaning motion.

STM32CI²CPWMUART
Firmware notes +

I implemented interrupt-driven UART diagnostics, sensor freshness checks, latched faults, and watchdog recovery. During bring-up of the standalone bumper-operated refill utility, I traced the signal from the bumper through driver enable and PWM to the pump, corrected the driver wake sequence, and verified physical actuation.

04ASSISTIVE ROBOTICS

Rollie Autonomous Walker

A motorized walker that combines semantic segmentation, stereo depth, and LiDAR to navigate pedestrian routes, follow a selected person, and drive to a nearby point. Its iPhone app offers spoken commands, VoiceOver, route previews, and explicit motion confirmation. The integrated walker was tested on physical hardware.

JetsonROS 2OAK-DESP32SwiftUI
Project notes +

The Jetson combines camera and LiDAR data into a local hazard map and routes motion requests through a single serial bridge. The ESP32 controls motor PWM and disables output if valid commands stop arriving. The app also supports manual driving and downloads timestamped sensor recordings.
